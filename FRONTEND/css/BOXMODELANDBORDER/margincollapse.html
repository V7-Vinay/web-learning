Understanding Margin Collapse in CSS

Adjacent Sibling Elements

Sibling 1: Margin 20px top & bottom
Sibling 2: Margin 20px top & bottom

Note: When two block-level siblings are stacked vertically, their adjacent vertical margins collapse. Instead of adding 20px + 20px (40px), only the larger margin (20px) is applied between them.

Parent and Child Margin Collapse (No Padding)

Parent element (no padding) with an external margin of 40px.

Child element with 30px margin on top and bottom.

Because the parent has no padding or border on its top edge, the top margin of the child may collapse with the parent's margin.

Parent and Child (With Padding)

Parent element (with 10px padding) with an external margin of 40px.

Child element with 30px margin on top and bottom.

With padding present, the parent's margin and child's margin do not collapse, so the full margins are maintained.
